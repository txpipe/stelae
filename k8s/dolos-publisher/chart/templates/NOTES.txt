{{ if .Values.schedule -}}
An image or values change takes effect at the next run. A run never starts
beside one still in flight, but a Job started by hand beside the CronJob is a
second writer: suspend the schedule and wait for its run to finish first.
So is the Job that clearing schedule, or a rollback to a chart before 0.5,
creates: suspend and wait for the run in flight to terminate before that
upgrade. The schedule requires Kubernetes 1.31 or later: before 1.31 a run
can start beside a failed run's terminating pod. An
image on a new Dolos profile pin starts a fresh lineage in the root's emptied
dolos repository, and leaves a lineage from before the diff chain, at the root
itself, alone (see the chart README).
{{- else -}}
Before an upgrade or rollback, stop the previous writer and wait for its pod
to terminate. Bump run for the new Job. An image on a new Dolos profile pin
starts a fresh lineage in the root's emptied dolos repository, and leaves a
lineage from before the diff chain, at the root itself, alone (see the chart
README).
{{- end }}
